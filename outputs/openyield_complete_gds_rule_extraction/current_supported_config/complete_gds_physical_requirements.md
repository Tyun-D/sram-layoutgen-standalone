# Complete GDS Physical Requirements

| requirement_id | requirement_area | minimum_requirement | target_stage | evidence_needed | still_not_signoff_boundary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| REQ_C2_01 | C2 pin access requirements | Every WL/BL/BR/control/VDD/GND/top-IO sink or source needed by current supported config must have geometry-backed access or a geometry-backed synthesized replacement pin. | C2 | Pin inventories updated to GEOMETRY_BACKED_PIN with bbox/layer/net ownership. | This does not imply DRC/LVS closure. |
| REQ_C3_01 | C3 floorplan requirements | Array must be the macro core; row, column, control, dummy, replica, and boundary structures must be placed relative to actual array pitch and edge access windows. | C3 | Updated floorplan report and region bboxes derived from actual geometry. | This does not imply congestion-free final routing. |
| REQ_C4_01 | C4 signal routing requirements | WL/BL/BR/control/clk/addr/din/dout/top-IO routes must exist as real GDS geometry between source and target access points. | C4 | Real geometry inventory with pin-to-pin routes and no contract-only dependencies for required nets. | This does not imply timing closure or global route optimality. |
| REQ_C5_01 | C5 power network requirements | VDD/GND must connect module rails to top-level straps/pins through real taps and vias; no contract-only power stitches may remain. | C5 | Power continuity inventory with geometry-backed rails, taps, and top power pins. | This does not imply EM/IR signoff. |
| REQ_C6_01 | C6 validation requirements | Net-to-shape evidence must refer to actual GDS shapes, and the repaired top GDS must parse, map, and preserve hierarchy consistently for current supported config. | C6 | Final geometry-backed audits and parsed GDS comparison. | This does not imply full LVS-clean or DRC-clean status unless separately proven. |
| REQ_C6_02 | complete GDS claim requirements | The minimum complete-GDS bar is geometry-backed connectivity for the current supported config across signal, power, and top pins. | C6 | Contract/approximate counts reduced to zero for required complete-GDS claim scope. | Complete GDS is not equivalent to timing signoff, DRC signoff, or LVS signoff. |
