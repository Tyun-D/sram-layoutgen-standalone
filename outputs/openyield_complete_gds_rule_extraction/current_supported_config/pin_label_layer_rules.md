# Pin Label Layer Rules

| rule_name | source_evidence | rule_description | required_geometry | required_metadata | c0_blockers_addressed | implemented_in_stage |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| GeometryBackedMinimum | hierarchy_layout.add_layout_pin / add_layout_pin_segment_center | A complete-GDS pin must have geometry bbox, layer, and stable net name. A label alone is insufficient. | Pin rectangle or routed pin segment on a real layer. | net name, bbox, layer, owning instance/module. | MISSING_PIN_GEOMETRY,MISSING_TOP_PIN_GEOMETRY | C2/C6 |
| SynthesizedPinAllowance | hierarchy_layout.copy_layout_pin / add_io_pin | Synthesized pins are only acceptable when they are copied or routed from real child geometry, not invented without a geometry source. | Child-backed geometry and optional escape-routed replacement pin. | source instance pin reference and replacement history. | MISSING_TOP_PIN_GEOMETRY | C2/C4/C6 |
| LabelOnlyBoundary | hierarchy_layout.add_label_pin | Label-only correspondence points can help debug LVS but cannot serve as complete-GDS connectivity evidence. | None; this is explicitly non-sufficient. | must be marked as label-only or correspondence-only. | MISSING_PIN_GEOMETRY | C6 |
| PinShapeLabelConsistency | hierarchy_layout.add_layout_pin / replace_layout_pin | Pin bbox, layer, label text, and net name must remain consistent when a pin is moved or replaced. | Final pin shape plus any retained internal route geometry. | pin-to-net and old-to-new pin mapping. | MISSING_LVS_READY_NET_MAPPING,MISSING_TOP_PIN_GEOMETRY | C4/C6 |
| NetToShapeTruth | hierarchy_layout.replace_layout_pin; signal_escape_router.replace_layout_pins | net_to_shape_map must point to real GDS shapes that survive into the final top cell, not to report-only bbox rows. | GDS-resident shapes with stable bbox and layer. | net_to_shape entry id, shape bbox, shape layer, source evidence path. | MISSING_LVS_READY_NET_MAPPING,PLACEHOLDER_VISUAL_GEOMETRY | C4/C6 |
