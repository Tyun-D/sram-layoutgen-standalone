# M12C3A Human Review Required Items

1. Check PINV A/Z pins lie on real accessible metal.
2. Check VDD/VSS rails are clear and aligned to reasonable cell boundaries.
3. Check NMOS/PMOS, implant, well, and tap relationships are visually sensible.
4. Check different-size PINV variants visibly differ in physical geometry.
5. Check TRANSMISSION_GATE IN/OUT/CTR_P/CTR_N pins are clearly distinguished.
6. Check TRANSMISSION_GATE contains one NMOS and one PMOS rather than proxy rectangles.
7. Check the atlas for obvious overlap, fracture, or empty-cell issues.
