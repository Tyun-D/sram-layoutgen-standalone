- Confirm only one GPT-approved minimal stage was executed.
- Confirm no forbidden paths were modified.
- Confirm no project ledger files were changed.
- Confirm no approved reusable GDS or OpenYield files were changed.
- Confirm whether any GDS was generated; if yes, the GPT instruction authorizing that exact stage must be cited.
- Export a report and a tar.gz evidence package.
- Fill in `HANDOFF_TEMPLATE.md`.
- Include exact commit IDs, rerun commands, and any conflicts.
- Include bundle/patch if direct push is unavailable.
- Stop and wait for GPT audit. Do not auto-advance.
